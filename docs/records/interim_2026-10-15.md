## Consequence seen in the re-runs

With unweighted validation, early stopping in the entropy arm picked a checkpoint from
inside the warmup in 2 of 4 cup runs (best epochs 3, 5) and 2 of 2 GA runs (5, 4): those
models are identical to the baseline of the same seed (same test Dice to 6 decimals:
cup runs 1, 4; GA runs 3, 8). After weighting starts, validation Dice rises
(e.g. cup run 1: 0.65 → 0.69, 0.70) but validation BCE does not fall, so those epochs are
never selected.

Interim means (soft Dice; n tiny, not for inference):

| | cup | GA |
|---|---|---|
| baseline | 0.726 (n=3) | 0.641 (n=2) |
| entropy γ5 W5 | 0.715 (n=4) | 0.641 (n=2) |

The previous pipeline's D12 parity verdict ("FAIL") compared runs stopped on unweighted
validation with paper-era runs stopped on weighted validation, so it does not bear on
pipeline faithfulness. Q1 of DECISIONS D17 replaces it.
